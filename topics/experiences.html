Spiritual experiences

Did God speak to me?

Also asked as: Did God send me a sign? · Was my vision spiritual? · How do I understand a mystical experience?

A powerful experience can be meaningful without proving one supernatural explanation. Discernment should remain humble, grounded and open to ordinary explanations.

How the traditions answer

Contemplative traditions

Many traditions test experiences by their long-term fruits—humility, compassion, truthfulness and steadiness—not by intensity alone.

Religious communities

Claims are usually interpreted within scripture, tradition and accountable community; private certainty is not automatically treated as authority over others.

Psychological and medical perspectives

Stress, sleep loss, substances, neurological conditions and mental-health conditions can shape unusual experiences and deserve consideration without shame.

Where they converge

Do not make dangerous or irreversible decisions because of a voice, sign or vision. If experiences are frightening, commanding or disrupting life, speak promptly with a trusted person and a qualified health professional.

A question to sit with

What lasting effect is this experience having on your safety, humility, relationships and daily responsibilities?
