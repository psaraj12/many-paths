Forgiveness

Should I forgive?

Also asked as: How can I forgive someone? · Does forgiveness mean forgetting?

Forgiveness can release vengeance without denying harm. It does not necessarily require forgetting, immediate reconciliation, restored trust or remaining unsafe.

How the traditions answer

Hindu, Buddhist and Jain ethics

Forbearance, non-hatred, compassion and nonviolence weaken cycles of injury, while accountability still matters.

Christianity

Forgiveness is central and connected to grace, repentance and reconciliation, but responsible boundaries and protection of the vulnerable remain necessary.

Judaism and Islam

Mercy and forgiveness are honored alongside justice, restitution and repentance; forgiving is not the erasure of moral responsibility.

Where they converge

Mercy and justice need not be enemies. Reconciliation requires more conditions than an inward decision to release vengeance.

A question to sit with

What boundary would make it safer to loosen resentment without denying what happened?
