Karma

What is karma?

Also asked as: Is everything caused by karma? · Does karma punish us?

Karma generally concerns intentional action and consequence; it is not simply fate, instant cosmic punishment or a license to blame victims.

How the traditions answer

Hindu traditions

Karma connects action, character and consequences across differing accounts of rebirth, grace, knowledge and liberation.

Buddhism

Intention is central. Karma shapes conditions but does not imply a permanent soul or that every event is deserved.

Jainism

Karma is described as binding the soul through action and passion; disciplined nonviolence and purification support liberation.

Where they converge

These traditions emphasize moral causation and responsibility but differ in metaphysics and mechanisms.

A question to sit with

What intention is shaping the action available to you now?
