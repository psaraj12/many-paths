Meditation

How do I meditate?

Also asked as: What is meditation? · How should a beginner meditate?

Meditation is a family of practices, not one universal technique. A simple, low-risk beginning is five minutes of relaxed attention to natural breathing.

How the traditions answer

Hindu traditions

Dhyana may involve concentration, mantra, devotion or inquiry, situated within ethical and philosophical disciplines.

Buddhist traditions

Practices include calm, insight, mindfulness and loving-kindness, with methods differing across Theravada, Mahayana and Vajrayana.

Contemplative theistic traditions

Christian, Jewish, Islamic and Sikh contemplative practices may center prayer, remembrance, sacred words, attentive reading or loving presence.

Where they converge

Regularity, ethical grounding and gentleness matter more than dramatic experiences. Stop and seek qualified support if a practice causes severe distress or disorientation.

A question to sit with

For five minutes, notice one breath at a time without forcing it; when attention wanders, return kindly.
