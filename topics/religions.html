Many religions

Are all religions the same?

Also asked as: Which religion is true? · Why are there many religions?

Religions are not all the same: they make genuinely different claims about God, self, liberation, history and authority. They also share important ethical and contemplative insights.

How the traditions answer

Exclusivist approaches

One revelation or path is held to be uniquely or finally true, though respectful coexistence can still be affirmed.

Inclusivist and pluralist approaches

Other paths may participate in truth, or several traditions may disclose different aspects of reality without becoming interchangeable.

Secular study

Religions can be studied historically and comparatively while suspending judgment about supernatural truth claims.

Where they converge

Respect does not require pretending contradictions do not exist. Fair comparison states each tradition in terms its informed practitioners recognize.

A question to sit with

Can you name one real agreement and one real disagreement without caricaturing either side?
