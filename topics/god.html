God and ultimate reality

Does God exist?

Also asked as: What is God? · Is there an ultimate reality?

There is no single spiritual definition of God: traditions range from personal creator, to non-dual reality, to disciplined non-theism.

How the traditions answer

Hindu traditions

They include devotion to personal forms of Ishvara, qualified and dualist theism, and Advaita’s non-dual Brahman beyond ordinary categories.

Judaism, Christianity and Islam

They affirm one creator who is distinct from creation yet present to it. Their descriptions of revelation, divine attributes and relationship differ.

Buddhism and secular thought

Buddhism generally does not require a creator God for liberation. Secular philosophy may suspend judgment or reject the claim while still exploring awe, ethics and meaning.

Where they converge

The traditions differ fundamentally, so neutrality means describing their claims accurately rather than blending them into one.

A question to sit with

What would count as a good reason for you to believe—or not believe—a claim about ultimate reality?
