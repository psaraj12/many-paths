Death and afterlife

What happens after death?

Also asked as: Is there life after death? · Should I fear death?

Traditions make different claims about death, and none can be presented here as established empirical certainty.

How the traditions answer

Hindu, Buddhist and Jain traditions

They commonly teach rebirth and liberation from its cycle, while disagreeing about the self that continues and the means of release.

Judaism, Christianity and Islam

They include resurrection, judgment and life with God, with substantial differences within and between the traditions.

Secular perspectives

Many hold that personal consciousness ends with brain function, locating continuity in influence, relationships and the larger life of nature.

Where they converge

Awareness of mortality can deepen ethical seriousness, gratitude and care for the living even when beliefs about afterlife differ.

A question to sit with

What deserves your attention now because life is finite?
