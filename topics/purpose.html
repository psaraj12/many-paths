Purpose and meaning

What is the purpose of life?

Also asked as: How do I find meaning? · Why am I here?

Traditions frame purpose as liberation, awakening, relationship with God, ethical service or meaning created through responsible living.

How the traditions answer

Hindu traditions

Dharma, ethical flourishing, devotion, knowledge and moksha form overlapping accounts of a meaningful life.

Buddhist traditions

The path aims at freedom from suffering and compassionate wisdom rather than discovering a creator-assigned role.

Theistic and humanist traditions

Theistic paths may understand purpose as loving God and neighbour; humanists build meaning through truth, relationship, creativity and contribution.

Where they converge

Purpose is expressed through repeated commitments, not only discovered in a single dramatic insight.

A question to sit with

Who or what becomes better when you use your abilities well?
