Inner peace

How can I find inner peace?

Also asked as: How can I calm my mind? · Why is my mind restless?

Inner peace is usually cultivated, not permanently acquired. It can coexist with grief, uncertainty and responsible action.

How the traditions answer

Hindu traditions

Equanimity may grow through disciplined action without clinging to results, devotion, meditation and insight into deeper identity.

Buddhism

Mindfulness, ethical living, compassion and seeing impermanence reduce the struggle added to unavoidable experience.

Theistic traditions

Prayer, trust, surrender, repentance, gratitude and supportive community can ground peace in relationship with God.

Where they converge

Peace is not passivity. Healthy traditions join inner steadiness with truthful and compassionate action.
