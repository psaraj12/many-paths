Suffering

Why do we suffer?

Also asked as: What is the meaning of suffering? · Why is life painful?

Spiritual traditions rarely give one explanation for every kind of suffering; they distinguish natural pain, harmful action, attachment, injustice and mystery.

How the traditions answer

Buddhism

The Four Noble Truths diagnose dukkha, trace much of it to craving and ignorance, and teach a path that transforms our relationship to experience.

Hindu traditions

Explanations may include karma, ignorance of our deeper nature, and the instability of worldly life. Karma should not be used to blame a person who suffers.

Christian, Jewish and Islamic thought

Suffering may arise from freedom, injustice and a finite world. Lament, compassionate action, patience and trust are emphasized; some suffering remains an unanswered mystery.

Where they converge

Most traditions value compassionate response more than abstract explanation and reject indifference to preventable suffering.
