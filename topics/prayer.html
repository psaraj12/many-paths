Prayer

Does prayer work?

Also asked as: How should I pray? · Why pray?

Prayer may be petition, praise, confession, gratitude, remembrance, listening or inner transformation; claims about external outcomes require humility.

How the traditions answer

Hindu traditions

Puja, mantra, bhajan and personal prayer cultivate devotion and relationship with the divine in diverse forms.

Judaism, Christianity, Islam and Sikhism

Regular prayer joins worship, moral formation and community. Petition is present, but prayer is not merely a technique for controlling events.

Buddhist traditions

Chanting, aspiration, recollection and dedication of merit can shape intention and compassion without necessarily addressing a creator deity.

Where they converge

Prayer can change attention, intention and relationship. It should not replace necessary medical, practical or ethical action.

A question to sit with

Try expressing gratitude, honesty and one intention without demanding a particular outcome.
